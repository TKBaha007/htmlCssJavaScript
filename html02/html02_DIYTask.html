Recipe: creamy Mac'N'Cheese

This one of one mac n cheese is best paired with BBQ ribs
It is leaps and bounds ahead of the boxed versions.
This recipe has one powerful advantage for the cook.
There’s no need to preboil the pasta.
It cooks in the oven, absorbing the liquid from the dairy products.

steps to make

  1. Set oven to 375 degrees and position the oven rack in the upper 1/3 of the ovan
  2. Use 1 tablespoon butter to grease a 9-inch round or square baking pan.
  3. In a blender, purée cottage cheese, milk, mustard, cayenne, nutmeg and salt and pepper.
  4. Reserve ¼ cup grated Cheddar for topping. In a large bowl, combine remaining grated Cheddar, milk mixture and uncooked pasta.
  5. Pour into prepared pan, cover tightly with foil and bake 30 minutes.
  6. Uncover pan, stir gently, sprinkle with reserved cheese and dot with remaining tablespoon butter.
  7. Bake, uncovered, 30 minutes more, until browned.
  8. Let cool at least 15 minutes before serving.

Ingredents

  1. unsalted butter
  2. cottage cheese
  3. milk (not skim)
  4. dry mustard
  5. ground cayenne
  6. ground nutmeg
  7. kosher salt
  8. black pepper
  9. sharp or extra-sharp Cheddar, grated
  10. elbow pasta, uncooked

Mesurements

  1. 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  2. 1 cup cottage cheese (not low-fat)
  3. 2 cups milk (not skim)
  4. 1 teaspoon dry mustard
  5. Pinch of ground cayenne
  6. Pinch of ground nutmeg
  7. ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  8. ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  9. 1 pound sharp or extra-sharp Cheddar, grated
  10. ½ pound elbow pasta, uncooked

Non ingredent requirerments

  • 9-inch round or square baking pan
  • Measuring cups
  • blender
  • tablespoon
  • food scale
