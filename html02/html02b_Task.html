Planning Your Party

Instructions

    Timeline

  1. Choose a date and venue (4-6 weeks before)
  2. Create and send invitations (3 weeks before)
  3. Plan the menu and purchase ingredients (1 week before)
  4. Decorate and set up (1 day before)
  5. Enjoy the party! (Day of event)

    Guest Essentials

  1. Comfortable seating
  2. Good lighting
  3. Beverage station
  4. Music or entertainment
  5. Clean restrooms

    Food Preparation

  • 1. Prepare appetizers (with nested items: Arrange on platters, Chill until serving)
  • 2. Cook main dishes (with nested items: Set oven temperature, Monitor cooking times)
  • 3. Prepare desserts (with nested items: Bake ahead if possible, Keep refrigerated)
  • 4. Set up the drink station

    Party Supplies Checklist

    • Plates: Paper or ceramic, 1 per guest plus extras
    • Cups: 2-3 per guest (for multiple drinks)
    • Napkins: 4-5 per guest
  2. Utensils: Forks, spoons, knives for each guest
  3. Trash bags: 3-4 large bags for cleanup
