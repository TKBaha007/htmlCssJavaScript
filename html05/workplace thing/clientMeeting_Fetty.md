 4. Branding & Design Assets

<h3> Existing Branding</h3>

 **Do you have existing brand guidelines?**
 [ ] Yes (Full brand book with fonts, color codes, and assets)* [ ] Partial (Logo and basic colors only)
 [ ] No (We need help developing branding—see "Starting from Scratch" below)


 **Logo Files:** Please provide your logo as an SVG file if you have one. *(An SVG is made of shapes instead of pixels, so it stays sharp at any size. A PNG is the next best choice. A blurry JPG copied from social media is hard to work with.)*
 **Exact Colors:** What are your exact brand color codes (e.g., `#1E6FD9`)?
 **Fonts:** Which fonts do you currently use on your signs, menus, print materials, or business cards?
 **Other Materials:** Can you share physical or digital marketing materials (business cards, flyers, menus) that showcase your current style?

 <h3>Situation: Starting Branding from Scratch</h3>

*(If you do not have an established logo or brand guide, please answer the following)*

 **What three words describe how you want customers to feel about your business?**
 **Are there colors you love or colors you definitely want to avoid?**
 **Are there logos from other businesses that you like? What specifically do you like about them?**
 **Should your logo include a symbol/picture, just the business name, or both?**
